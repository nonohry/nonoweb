## Teaching Philosophy

I believe in hands-on, project-based learning where students apply theoretical concepts to real-world problems. My courses emphasize:

- **Active Learning**: Interactive lectures with coding exercises
- **Real Projects**: Working with actual datasets and problems
- **Collaborative Learning**: Team projects and peer learning
- **Research Integration**: Incorporating current research into coursework
