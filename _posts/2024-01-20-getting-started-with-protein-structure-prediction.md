# Getting Started with Protein Structure Prediction

Protein structure prediction has been revolutionized by deep learning methods like AlphaFold. In this post, I'll walk you through the basics of computational approaches to this fascinating problem.

## Why Protein Structure Matters

Understanding protein structure is crucial for:
- Drug discovery and design
- Understanding disease mechanisms
- Enzyme engineering
- Synthetic biology applications

## Traditional Approaches

Before deep learning, researchers relied on:

1. **Homology Modeling**: Using known structures of similar proteins
2. **Threading**: Fitting sequences to known structural templates
3. **Ab Initio Methods**: Physics-based simulations from first principles

## The Deep Learning Revolution

Modern methods use:
- **Transformer Architectures**: Attention mechanisms capture long-range interactions
- **Multiple Sequence Alignments**: Evolutionary information guides predictions
- **End-to-End Learning**: Direct prediction from sequence to 3D coordinates
