# Welcome to My Academic Blog

I'm excited to launch this blog as a space to share thoughts on research, teaching, and developments in computer science and computational biology.

On this blog, you'll find:

- **Research Updates**: Insights from ongoing projects and recent publications
- **Teaching Resources**: Materials and reflections from my courses
- **Conference Reports**: Summaries and highlights from academic conferences
- **Technical Tutorials**: Guides on machine learning, bioinformatics tools, and data analysis
- **Career Advice**: Tips for students and early-career researchers
