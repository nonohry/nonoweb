# Research Interests

## Current Projects

### 1. Deep Learning for Protein Structure Prediction
Using transformer architectures and attention mechanisms to predict 3D protein structures from amino acid sequences. This project aims to accelerate drug discovery and understand disease mechanisms.

**Funding**: NIH R01 Grant ($2.5M, 2023-2028)  
**Collaborators**: Stanford University, MIT

### 2. Machine Learning in Clinical Diagnostics
Developing interpretable AI models for early disease detection using electronic health records and medical imaging data.

**Funding**: NSF CAREER Award ($500K, 2022-2027)  
**Collaborators**: Mayo Clinic, Johns Hopkins

### 3. Genomics Data Analysis
Novel statistical methods for analyzing large-scale genomic datasets, with applications to cancer research and personalized medicine.

**Funding**: DOE Grant ($1.2M, 2024-2027)
